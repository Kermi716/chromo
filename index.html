About Chromo LTD

Founded in early 2023, Chromo LTD is an independent artificial intelligence distribution company based in the United Kingdom. For more than 3 years, we have provided seamless, unified access to premier frontier intelligence.

Company Story

Direct access to leading AI models for over 3 years

Chromo was established to solve model fragmentation. Rather than managing separate subscriptions and restrictive rate limits across multiple vendors, our infrastructure unifies the world's most capable neural networks into a single, cohesive workspace.

From our initial beta in 2023 to our current high-throughput platform, we have connected researchers, engineers, and creators to frontier models including Claude 5.5, Gemini 4.0, and GPT Astra with zero prompt retention and private throughput.

Terms & Privacy

Zero Training Retention

Chromo LTD maintains strict data isolation. We do not store, log, or use any prompts, chat histories, code snippets, or attached files to train or tune future neural models.

In-Flight Memory Processing

All user inputs are processed strictly in volatile memory and encrypted using TLS 1.3 in transit. Upon token streaming completion, your session data is cleared from intermediate routing servers.

Account Credential Protection

User accounts are protected through salted cryptographic hashes. We never share account identifiers or subscription details with third-party advertisers.

Data Subject Rights

Under UK and EU GDPR, you retain the full right to request complete account deletion and data export at any time by contacting privacy@chromo.ai.

Subscription Activation

Access to Spark, Pulse, and Nova subscription plans is granted immediately upon successful payment verification and remains valid for the selected calendar duration.

Fair Usage Standards

Users agree to utilize Chromo compute capabilities within reasonable non-automated usage parameters and refrain from deploying automated scrapers, denial-of-service traffic, or malicious payload generation.

Model Availability

Chromo acts as a high-speed routing gateway to third-party foundation models. While we guarantee high-priority allocation, individual model availability is aligned with underlying upstream provider operations.

Transparent Billing

Plans do not contain hidden recurring fees or auto-escalating tariffs. Subscriptions simply conclude at the end of the chosen timeframe unless manually renewed by the account holder.
