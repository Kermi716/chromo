About Chromo

Started in 2023 in London, Chromo gives developers, researchers, and teams a single interface for working with top models from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI.

One account instead of three subscriptions

Switching between separate tabs and paying $20/month for every AI provider gets expensive fast. We built Chromo so you can switch between Claude, Gemini, and GPT inside the same chat thread whenever a task calls for a different model.

We route requests directly through enterprise API tiers. That means your prompts, uploaded files, and code snippets are never used to train upstream models, and you don't get hit with sudden regional blocks.

Terms & Privacy

Zero Training Retention

We do not use your prompts, chat histories, code, or uploaded files to train AI models. All requests are sent through commercial API agreements with zero-retention policies.

Encryption & Storage

Traffic between your browser and our servers is encrypted via TLS 1.3. Chat history is stored locally in your browser so you stay in control of your conversations.

Account Security

Passwords are stored as salted cryptographic hashes. We do not sell user data or share account details with marketing third parties.

Account Deletion

You can request full account removal at any time by emailing privacy@chromo.ai.

Subscriptions

Access to Spark, Pulse, and Nova plans activates immediately after payment confirmation and remains active for the purchased period.

Acceptable Use

Accounts are meant for individual use. Automated scraping, credential sharing, or abusing API rate limits may result in account suspension.

Service Availability

Chromo routes requests to upstream providers. If a specific model experiences downtime on the provider's side, you can switch to any other available model in your plan.

No Hidden Renewals

All plans are one-time prepaid terms. We never charge recurring fees automatically—you only renew when you choose to.
